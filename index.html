Put on headphones

Headphones keep the click out of the microphone, so your timing is measured cleanly. Wired headphones are best. Bluetooth adds a delay that differs from device to device, and the last step corrects for it.

SyncVox listens to your voice to check timing and pitch. The sound is analysed on this phone and is not uploaded anywhere.

Sa is the note your scale is built on. Pick one that is comfortable to sing. You can change it any time in the Pitch tab, and Detect Sa there can find it from your voice.

Your phone and headphones delay the click a little. A 10-second clap test measures that delay, so “on the beat” really means on the beat. Wear the headphones you will sing with.

You can also do this later in Settings.

          How to write the notes
          • Sargam: Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni. A small first letter or a flat sign is komal: re ga dha ni, Re♭. Tivra Ma is Ma#.
          • Octaves: add ' for the higher octave and , for the lower one: Sa', Pa,.
          • Western: C D E, with # or b, and an octave number if you like: F#3, Bb4.
          • Time: every word is one beat. Re:2 lasts two beats, - holds the last note one more beat, . is a rest, | is ignored.
          • You can also write in Hindi letters: सा रे ग म प ध नि.

        Tuning tips
        
                  
          
                • Play one string at a time and let it ring. Hold the phone close to the instrument, in a quiet room.
                  • 
          
                • Always tune up to the note. If the string is too high, loosen it past the note, then tighten slowly until the needle sits in the green.
                  • 
          
                • Detect it picks the nearest string for you. Choose I choose and tap a peg to lock onto one string, which helps when a string is very far off.
                  • 
          
                • Any note works for instruments that are not listed, such as a keyboard or a flute.
                  • 
          
                • Concert pitch is 440 Hz for almost everything. Change it (432 to 445 Hz) only if your group tunes to something else.
                  • 
          
                • Capo: pick the fret your capo is on and the targets rise to match, so you can tune with it clamped.
                  • 
          
                • Locked appears when you hold the note within 3 cents for half a second.
