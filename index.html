        Tips for better readings
        • Wear headphones. A click from the speaker leaks into the mic and can count as a note.
        • Sing with consonants such as la, da or ta. Soft humming has no clear start, though a change of pitch inside a hum is also picked up.
        • Latency shift: if you feel on the beat but always read about the same amount late, raise the shift until the average sits near zero. Bluetooth headphones add a lot of delay.
        • Half-beats: choose this when you sing two notes per click, otherwise the second note is scored against the wrong beat.
        • Microphone blocked? Some in-app views do not allow the mic. Open the page directly in your browser and allow it, or switch to Tap and tap on each sung syllable.
